# Piece images

The SVGs in this folder are the "cburnett" chess pieces by
[Colin M.L. Burnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User:Cburnett), unmodified, taken from
[lichess-org/lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/cburnett)
(`public/piece/cburnett`).
